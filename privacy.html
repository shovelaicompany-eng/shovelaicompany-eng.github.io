ShovelAI Channel Manager – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Who we are

ShovelAI Channel Manager is a private internal tool operated by ShovelAI LLC, a California single-member limited liability company. It is used only by ShovelAI LLC to manage the company's own YouTube channel, The Collector's Chair (@collectorschair), a fragrance channel.

This application is not offered to the public and has no third-party users.

Contact: shovelaicompany@gmail.com

What this app does

The app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • Read statistics for The Collector's Chair channel
  • Edit metadata for the company's own videos, including titles, descriptions, tags, and playlists
  • Upload videos as private drafts to that channel

Information we access and how we use it

When authorized through Google OAuth, the app may access YouTube account and channel data needed to manage The Collector's Chair, including channel identity, video metadata, upload capability, and channel statistics.

We use this information solely to operate and manage ShovelAI LLC's own YouTube channel. We do not use it for advertising, for analytics sold to others, or for any purpose unrelated to managing that channel.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and related credentials are stored only on ShovelAI LLC's own computer, used only for this purpose, and are not shared with third parties.

We do not sell or share data

We do not sell, rent, or share Google or YouTube user data with third parties. We do not allow third parties to access this data, except when required by law.

Compliance

ShovelAI Channel Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. Google's own practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, the app can no longer access the Google or YouTube account until it is authorized again. Stored tokens are then no longer usable.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to shovelaicompany@gmail.com.
